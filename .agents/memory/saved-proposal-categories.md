name: Saved proposal categories
description: Which investment proposal workflows must remain distinct and visible in Saved Clients.
---

The Saved Clients drawer must include FinFriend Planner, SIP optimizer, SIF research, PMS, and AIF proposals. SIP and SIF are separate workflows; a proposal containing multiple research categories should identify all of them.

**Why:** The user explicitly confirmed that both SIP and SIF proposals, along with PMS and AIF proposals, belong in the drawer.

**How to apply:** Preserve the correct report category and open route when saving or listing each proposal. Do not treat SIF as a spelling variant of SIP.
